# 🎬 Aetheris Hackathon Demo Video Script (3.5 Minutes)
> **Recommended Screen Recording Plan to score maximum points with the Jury**

---

### **[0:00 - 0:35] Scene 1: The Problem & The Setup**
* **Visual on Screen:** Show the Aetheris studio interface (`http://localhost:3000`).
* **Speaker Script:**
  > *"Hello judges! Traditional online education gives students boring pre-recorded monologues or text chatbots that only answer questions. Real teaching requires an adaptive human-like educator who plans, demonstrates, interacts, and fixes misconceptions on the fly. Introducing **Aetheris**."*
* **Action:**
  - Upload a PDF or type the prompt: *"Fundamentals of Electric Circuits & Ohm's Law"*.
  - Select **Time: 5 Minutes**, **Level: Beginner**, **Language: Hinglish**.
  - Click **"Launch Live AI Classroom Session"**.

---

### **[0:35 - 1:45] Scene 2: The Synchronized Video & Smart Blackboard**
* **Visual on Screen:** The split-screen classroom opens. The AI Avatar starts speaking natural Hinglish with glowing audio waves and emotion badges.
* **On the Blackboard:** The equation $V = I \times R$ renders in crisp KaTeX, followed by an animated Mermaid diagram showing a water pump and valve analogy.
* **Speaker Script:**
  > *"Notice what's happening here: this is NOT a simple talking head reading bullet points. Aetheris is orchestrating a synchronized multi-track lesson. On the left, our AI professor explains the concept in natural Hinglish. On the right, the digital smartboard actively renders mathematical derivations and hydraulic analogy workflows in perfect sync with the voice."*

---

### **[1:45 - 2:45] Scene 3: Socratic Interactivity & Misconception Diagnosis (Winning Moment)**
* **Visual on Screen:** Beat 3 reaches an interactive checkpoint. The video pauses. The modal pops up asking: *"If Resistance increases while Voltage is constant, what happens to Current?"*
* **Action:** Click the microphone button or type a common student mistake: *"Current increases because resistance gives it more push."*
* **Click "Submit Understanding"**.
* **On Screen:** The AI triggers the **Misconception Diagnostic Badge**:
  - Root Cause: *Confusing direct proportionality with fraction denominators.*
  - Teacher's Spoken Analogy: *"Think of a water pipe being squeezed..."*
  - The blackboard instantly updates with a remedial diagram.
* **Speaker Script:**
  > *"This is our key differentiator. When a student makes a mistake, Aetheris doesn't just display 'Incorrect'. It diagnoses the cognitive root cause, explains the mistake using an intuitive physical metaphor, updates the blackboard with a remedial diagram, and verifies understanding before moving forward."*
* **Action:** Click **"Apply Remediation & Resume Lesson"**.

---

### **[2:45 - 3:20] Scene 4: Code Implementation, Assessment & 7-Day Spaced Retention**
* **Visual on Screen:** The lesson completes and transitions into the **Mastery Analytics Dashboard**.
* **On Screen:**
  - Concept Mastery Score: **85%**.
  - Mastered vs. Remediated concepts highlighted.
  - Interactive **7-Day Personalized Spaced Retention Plan**.
  - Click **"Download Full Blackboard Notes & Report"** to show instant TXT/PDF note generation.
* **Speaker Script:**
  > *"At the end of the lesson, Aetheris generates a full mastery scorecard, highlights weak areas, gives the student their complete blackboard notes, and builds an adaptive 7-day spaced retention study plan so learning sticks."*

---

### **[3:20 - 3:35] Scene 5: Conclusion & Architecture Wrap-up**
* **Visual on Screen:** Quick 5-second flash of the system architecture diagram in the README.
* **Speaker Script:**
  > *"Powered by Google Gemini, grounded RAG, Edge-TTS, and a reactive Next.js smartboard engine, Aetheris is the AI Teacher of the Future. Thank you!"*
